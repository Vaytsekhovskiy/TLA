This specification models a bounded buffer shared by nonempty producer and
consumer sets. The state consists of a sequence-valued buffer and sets of
waiting producers and consumers. A producer waits when the buffer is full or
appends its identity otherwise; a consumer waits when the buffer is empty or
removes the oldest item otherwise. Successful operations may remove one
identity from the opposite waiting set. Producers and consumers are disjoint,
the capacity is at least one, and the invariant states that not all producers
and all consumers are simultaneously waiting.
